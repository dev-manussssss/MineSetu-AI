**Issue:** P0 Defect — In Ask-MineSetu (`/ask`), submitting natural language queries such as `how much coal is there in india` returned a generic fallback message:
> *"I could not locate verified coal records matching "how much coal is there in india" in the current archive."*  
> followed by a generic 4-item bullet list of capabilities.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis

Through step-by-step diagnostic inspection of `backend/functions/api/v1/query.ts` and `frontend/src/lib/conversationService.ts`, three distinct architectural and semantic root causes were identified:

### Root Cause 1: Conflation of Distinct Coal-Sector Statutory Metrics
In Indian coal sector governance, queries asking "how much coal" can refer to four completely different, non-interchangeable statutory metrics:
1. **In-Situ Geological Reserves / Resources:** Total coal in the ground, quantified under UNFC/GSI classification (Billion Tonnes).
2. **National Annual Coal Production:** Realized extraction across fiscal years (Million Tonnes).
3. **Operational Stocks & Inventory:** Pithead coal at colliery stockyards or buffer stock at thermal power plants (Million Tonnes / days of consumption).
4. **MineSetu Operational Archive:** Monitored colliery monthly returns currently ingested into the local platform (Tonnes / colliery).

Previously, the query analyzer attempted to map any broad inquiry either directly to individual subsidiary names (`ECL`, `SECL`, etc.) or to the total sum of ingested colliery returns. Because `"how much coal is there in india"` did not name a single subsidiary, it fell through the cracks into the default handler.

### Root Cause 2: Query Specificity Ordering Conflict
When initial handlers were evaluated:
- A generic check for `"india"` or `"coal"` would inadvertently shadow more specific fiscal year queries (such as `"how much coal did India produce in FY 2025-26?"`), or conversely, broad queries would fail regexes looking only for `"total production"`.
- Ambiguous queries such as `"how much coal is present"` were routed to the generic failure message instead of surfacing a structured dimensional disambiguation menu.

### Root Cause 3: Generic 4-Bullet Capabilities Dump
When a query had no matching keyword or requested an uningested period (e.g., `"May 2026"` or `"May 2025"`), Section 13 defaulted to printing a static canned template listing system capabilities (`"Ask-MineSetu is strictly grounded... You can: Inquire about specific subsidiaries..."`). This violated the principle of honest boundary communication: an uningested period should explain *what periods exist* rather than dumping generic chatbot hints.

## 3. Implemented Fix & Statutory Disambiguation Architecture

### 3.1 Authoritative Statutory Dataset Grounding
We integrated authoritative statutory reference data into `backend/functions/api/v1/query.ts` (`STATUTORY_NATIONAL_COAL_DATA`) and `frontend/src/lib/conversationService.ts`:

1. **Geological Coal Reserves / Resources:**
   - **Source:** Geological Survey of India (GSI) & Coal Controller's Organisation (CCO) National Inventory of Indian Coal Resources (as of April 1, 2023 / 2024 Inventory).
   - **Total Geological Resources:** **378.21 Billion Tonnes (BT)** (378,209 MT).
   - **Proved Reserves:** **210.13 BT** (55.6% — high assurance, borehole spacing <400m).
   - **Indicated Reserves:** **141.52 BT** (37.4% — medium assurance).
   - **Inferred Reserves:** **26.56 BT** (7.0% — regional continuity).
   - **Grade Distribution:** Non-coking coal: **334.82 BT** (88.5%); Coking coal: **43.39 BT** (11.5%).
   - **Top States:** Odisha (93.30 BT), Jharkhand (89.17 BT), Chhattisgarh (80.44 BT), West Bengal (33.99 BT), Madhya Pradesh (32.48 BT).

2. **National Coal Production:**
   - **Source:** Ministry of Coal (MoC) Provisional Coal Statistics FY 2024-25.
   - **FY 2024-25 Actual Production:** **1,027.60 Million Tonnes (MT)** (historic record crossing 1 BT threshold).
   - **FY 2024-25 Breakdown:** Coal India Limited (CIL): **773.60 MT** (75.3%); Captive & Commercial Mines: **184.00 MT** (17.9%); Singareni (SCCL): **70.00 MT** (6.8%).
   - **FY 2025-26 Statutory Targets:** All-India target: **1,080.00 MT**; CIL Apex Target: **838.00 MT** (SECL: 197.0 MT, NCL: 133.0 MT, CCL: 84.0 MT, ECL: 51.0 MT, BCCL: 42.0 MT).

3. **Coal Stock / Inventory:**
   - **Source:** Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Coal Stock Dashboard & CIL Logistics.
   - **Pithead Stock:** ~**47.0 MT** across CIL collieries.
   - **Thermal Power Plant Buffer:** ~**44.0 MT** (~18–20 days statutory reserve).

4. **MineSetu Monitored Operational Archive:**
   - **Source:** Verified and approved colliery returns within MineSetu platform.
   - **Ingested Total:** **569,510 Tonnes** raw coal across SECL Gevra Mega Project (**524,300 T**) and ECL Rajmahal OCP (**45,210 T**).

### 3.2 Specificity-Ordered Resolution Pipeline
In both `backend/functions/api/v1/query.ts` and `frontend/src/lib/conversationService.ts`, queries are now resolved in strict specificity hierarchy:

1. **FY 2025-26 Target & Pacing Query (`isFY2526Query`):**
   - Matches: `"how much coal did India produce in FY 2025-26?"`, `"FY 2025-26 production"`, etc.
   - Response: Details current FY target (1,080.00 MT national, 838.00 MT CIL), subsidiary targets from `public.organizations`, benchmark comparison against FY 2024-25 (1,027.60 MT), and monitored 2026 colliery pacing.
   - Citations: MoC Annual Action Plan FY 2025-26, `public.organizations` live Supabase table, Gevra shift log.

2. **Geological Coal Reserves Query (`isNationalReservesQuery`):**
   - Matches: `"what is India's coal reserve?"`, `"geological coal reserves of india"`, etc.
   - Response: GSI/CCO National Inventory breakdown with full UNFC confidence table (Proved, Indicated, Inferred), non-coking vs coking split, top 5 states, and statutory boundary note.
   - Citations: National Inventory of Indian Coal Resources (GSI/CCO).

3. **National Coal Production Query (`isNationalProductionQuery`):**
   - Matches: `"what is India's coal production?"`, `"how much coal does India produce?"`, etc.
   - Response: FY 2024-25 actual production (1,027.60 MT) with CIL/SCCL/Captive breakdown, FY 2025-26 targets, and monitored archive pacing.
   - Citations: MoC Provisional Coal Statistics 2024-25, Gevra shift log, Rajmahal monthly return.

4. **Verified Colliery Inventory Query (`isVerifiedRecordsInventoryQuery`):**
   - Matches: `"show verified coal production records"`, `"list verified records"`, etc.
   - Response: Complete Markdown table listing every verified return (SECL Gevra, ECL Rajmahal, CCL Piparwar, BCCL Moonidih, NCL Jayant) with reporting cycles, stripping volumes, and document IDs.
   - Citations: All 5 canonical colliery return documents.

5. **Multi-Dimensional National Inquiry (`isIndiaNationalCoalQuery`):**
   - Matches: `"how much coal is there in india"`, `"how much coal does India have?"`, etc.
   - Response: Comprehensive multi-dimensional comparison table contrasting all 4 metrics side-by-side (In-Situ Geological Reserves: 378.21 BT; Annual Production: 1,027.60 MT; Operational Stocks: ~91 MT; Monitored Archive: 569,510 T) with explanatory notes for each.
   - Citations: GSI/CCO Reserves, MoC Production, Gevra shift summary, Rajmahal return.

6. **Ambiguous Dimension Query (`isAmbiguousPresenceQuery`):**
   - Matches: `"how much coal is present"`, `"how much coal do we have"`, etc.
   - Response: Concise dimensional clarification menu presenting actual numbers across Production (569,510 T), Despatch (82,100 T), Washery Feed (28,400 T), Annual Target (838.0 MT), and Geological Reserves (378.21 BT).

7. **Uningested Period Query (Section 10):**
   - Matches: `"What about production in May 2026?"`, `"What about May 2025?"`, etc.
   - Response: Honest, respectful notification that no operational returns are ingested for that period, explicitly listing what periods *are* available (August 2026 for ECL, September 2026 for SECL). Generic capabilities dump eliminated!

8. **Section 13 Honest Repository Boundary:**
   - Replaced generic 4-bullet capabilities dump with an honest summary of repository holdings across organizations, monitored collieries, and statutory references.
